### 🧠 About Me

I am an **AI & Machine Learning Developer** focused on building intelligent, data-driven models and autonomous **Agentic AI** workflows. My work spans from training foundational Machine Learning and Deep Learning pipelines to orchestrating multi-agent Generative AI systems capable of reasoning, tool use, and complex task execution.

- 🔭 **Core Focus:** Agentic AI Workflows, Large Language Models (LLMs), Deep Learning & Predictive Modeling
- 🛠️ **Engineering Approach:** Combining strong mathematical foundations in ML/DL with production-ready containerization and database architectures
- ⚡ **Current Exploration:** Multi-agent orchestration, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines, and autonomous AI tool-calling
